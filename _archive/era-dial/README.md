# The year dial

The classic site's way into the eras before the page peel: a tuning dial for
years, whose bar took on the look of each era. To put it back, replace these:

- `dial.html`: in `index.html`, everything from the `<!-- The way into the eras` comment through the `eraPeel` link.
- `dial.css`: in `style.css`, everything from `/* The way into the eras:` to the end of the file.
- `dial.js`: in `script.js`, everything from the `The way into the eras` comment block to the end of the file.
