The following is documentation regarding our Howdy! software program.


How do I make an Internet postcard?

A: Use Howdy! to make your postcard, adding your own image and text. Press the "Preview" button to see what you card will look like. When you are satisfied, press the "Ship It" button. Next press the "Send to the Web" button. Howdy! will prepare your postcard for the WEB. Then name your postcard whatever you wish (the extension will be "RDZ" and cannot be changed). Howdy! will attempt to start your email program and compose an email to send your postcard to us. If Howdy! is unable to start your email program, you MUST send your postcard to the following address and type in the subject as shown below :

To : howdyweb@altavista.com
Subject : postcard

When your site is ready you will receive an email confirmation with the path to your new site. The entire process happens very fast. Depending on Web traffic and your location, it can take as little as 10 minutes.

See How do I view my Internet postcard? for information on how to view your Internet postcard.

How do I view my postcard on the Internet?

A: See How do I make an Internet postcard? for information on creating an Internet postcard. When your site is ready you will receive an email confirmation with the path to your new site. Typically, the URL is:

www.altavista.com/howdy/your-email-address.

Where your-email-address will be YOUR email address (e.g. joe@home.com). You need to access the Internet using your favorite browser and type in the URL we mailed you. You postcard will be displayed. You can email the URL to all your friends and tell them to visit YOUR web site!

How do I make my postcards smaller so it transmits faster?

A: The biggest factors affecting postcard size are, in order, videos, audios and images.

Howdy! supports videos in both AVI and MPEG formats. MPEG videos are much, much smaller than AVI videos and should be used when possible. Unfortunately, MPEG playback requires special software and possibly hardware and the person you're sending your postcard to may not be able to play the video. AVI format is more common and playback is supported on all Windows platforms.

Howdy! supports audios in WAV format. Follow the guidelines in How do I make my audios as small as possible? and How can I reduce the size of an existing audio? when including an audio in your postcard.

Howdy! supports over 34 different image formats so you can import images from many sources. When your postcard is created your selected image is saved as a JPEG file; this provides the best compression available. The only way to further reduce the size of the image is to cut-out or crop only the portion of the image you are interested in. Use the CROP button in the Image Dialog to do this.
