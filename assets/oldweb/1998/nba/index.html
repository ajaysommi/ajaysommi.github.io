NBA legend Rick Barry is among the featured players in this week's edition of Inside Stuff. See a sneak preview and a lineup of the show.

All-Star Weekend Canceled
The NBA announced Tuesday the cancellation of the 1999 All-Star Weekend, scheduled for February 12-14 in Philadelphia, due to the continuing lockout. NBA Commissioner David Stern and Deputy Commissioner Russ Granik discussed the latest development in a media teleconference.

  R E L A T E D
On Dec. 3, Commissioner Stern said that all-day talks in New York had yielded "little or no progress."

On Oct. 28, the NBA indefinitely postponed the '98-99 season.

The NBA has announced a ticket-refund policy for games missed due to the lockout.

Liberty Name Adubato
Richie Adubato, who has nineteen years of NBA experience as a head and assistant coach, was named as the new head coach of the WNBA's New York Liberty Wednesday.

Finger Rollin'
Cool as "Ice," Spurs legend George Gervin, one of the most prolific scorers in basketball history, answered NBA.com fan questions in a live chat Dec. 4.

  R E L A T E D
In an exclusive interview, Gervin waxed nostalgic on the ABA, Dr. J and his legendary scoring race with David Thompson.

Folkl, Lynx Introduced
Kristin Folkl, who will star with the WNBA's Minnesota Lynx discussed a myriad of topics in a live chat on Monday.

  R E L A T E D
The WNBA expansion franchise in Minnesota unveiled its nickname -- the Lynx -- and accompanying logo on Dec. 5.
